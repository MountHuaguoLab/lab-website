Team

Jianwei Zheng is a Professor and Doctoral Supervisor. He received his Ph.D. in Engineering from Zhejiang University of Technology in June 2010 (Supervisor: Professor Wanliang Wang) and joined the School of Computer Science and Technology in July of the same year. His main research interests include intelligent modeling, data analysis, and AI4Science.

He has led 2 projects funded by the National Natural Science Foundation of China, 1 project from the National Key R&D Program, 1 project from Zhejiang Province's "Leading Goose" Program, 1 key project from the Provincial Natural Science Foundation, and 3 general projects from the Provincial Natural Science Foundation, along with 6 enterprise-commissioned projects. He has received the Second Prize for Scientific and Technological Progress in Zhejiang Province, the First Prize for Wu Wenjun Artificial Intelligence Science and Technology Progress Award, the Second Prize for Invention and Creation from the China Invention Association, and the Excellent Award for China Industry-University-Research Cooperation Innovation Achievements. He was selected as one of the World's Top 2% Scientists in 2025.

As the first or corresponding author, he has published over 100 papers in renowned domestic and international academic journals and conferences such as TNNLS, TIP, NeurIPS, CVPR, AAAI, ACM MM, TGRS, TCSVT, TMM, Pattern Recognition, Journal of Computer Research and Development, Journal of Software, and Acta Automatica Sinica, including 4 highly cited papers and 1 hot paper. He has received one International Conference Best Paper Award and one Best Poster Award. He has published 1 monograph. He holds over 30 national authorized invention patents, 2 utility model patents, and over 30 software copyrights as the first author.

He serves as the Deputy Director of the Zhejiang Provincial Big Data and Artificial Intelligence Professional Committee, an expert for the China International College Students' Innovation Competition, an executive committee member of the Zhejiang Provincial College Students' Artificial Intelligence Competition, a communication review expert for the National Natural Science Foundation of China, and a communication review expert for provincial foundations in Zhejiang and Guangdong. He previously served as a member of the Artificial Intelligence Professional Committee of the Zhejiang Computer Society and a director of the Hangzhou Artificial Intelligence Society.
